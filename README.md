### **Tableau | Data Visualization | Real Estate Analytics | Market Analysis**

### Project Overview

Analyzed real estate housing data using Tableau to explore property characteristics, house price distributions, and market trends.

This project focuses on interactive data visualization, market exploration, and comparative analysis to understand factors associated with house prices and identify meaningful patterns within the real estate market.

## Business Problem

The real estate market involves multiple property characteristics that influence housing prices and purchasing decisions.

Buyers, sellers, and real estate professionals need to understand property value patterns, compare market conditions, and evaluate pricing trends to make informed decisions.

This project explores housing data through Tableau visualizations to support property price analysis, market comparison, and data-driven decision-making.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze house price distributions and real estate market patterns.
- Explore relationships between property characteristics and house prices.
- Identify variations in property values across available categories.
- Compare housing market trends using interactive visualizations.
- Present meaningful insights to support property evaluation and market understanding.

## Project Highlights

1. **Data Exploration:** Examined the housing dataset to understand its structure, available attributes, and relevant real estate information.
2. **Data Preparation:** Worked with housing data to organize relevant information for effective analysis and visualization.
3. **House Price Analysis:** Explored house price distributions to understand variations in property values.
4. **Property Characteristics Analysis:** Investigated available property attributes to understand their relationship with housing prices.
5. **Comparative Market Analysis:** Compared property values across available categories to identify differences in market patterns.
6. **Data Visualization:** Developed Tableau visualizations to represent housing data, price distributions, and analytical patterns.
7. **Interactive Dashboard:** Used Tableau dashboard capabilities to present real estate insights in an organized and interactive format.
8. **Market Insights:** Interpreted visual patterns to support property evaluation, pricing comparisons, and real estate market understanding.
