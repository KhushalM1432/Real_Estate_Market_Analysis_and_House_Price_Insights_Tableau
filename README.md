## Real Estate Market Analysis & House Price Insights

## Data_Science

### **Tableau | Data Analytics | Business Intelligence | Real Estate Analytics | Interactive Dashboard Development**

## 1. Executive Summary

Developed an interactive Tableau-based Real Estate Market Analysis and House Price Insights Dashboard to transform housing data into meaningful business intelligence. The project focuses on analyzing residential property prices, identifying pricing patterns, examining property characteristics, and exploring geographical variations to support data-driven decision-making within the real estate industry.

Leveraged Tableau's data visualization and dashboard capabilities to present housing market information through interactive charts, geographical mapping, comparative analysis, and dynamic filters. The solution enables users to explore property values across different dimensions, including construction year, living area, lot size, bedrooms, bathrooms, property views, and condition.

The dashboard provides a consolidated analytical environment that helps real estate stakeholders explore market behavior, evaluate property characteristics, compare pricing patterns, and gain actionable insights for property assessment and market research.

## 2. Business Problem

Real estate pricing is influenced by multiple property-related factors, geographical characteristics, and market conditions. Understanding these factors is essential for buyers, sellers, investors, property analysts, and real estate professionals.

However, analyzing housing datasets without structured visualization can make it difficult to identify pricing patterns, compare property characteristics, evaluate geographical differences, and communicate meaningful findings.

The primary business challenge addressed by this project was to transform housing data into an interactive business intelligence solution that simplifies property price analysis and supports informed real estate decisions.

The dashboard addresses the following business questions:

- How do house prices fluctuate over time?
- What does the distribution of residential property prices reveal about the market?
- How do property characteristics such as bedrooms, bathrooms, and living area vary across properties?
- How do property view ratings and condition categories compare in terms of average house prices?
- How can geographical visualization help explore housing market distribution?
- How can users dynamically filter housing data to investigate specific property segments?

## 3. Project Objectives

- Perform exploratory data analysis (EDA) to understand housing market characteristics and pricing distributions.
- Analyze residential property price trends over time.
- Examine the distribution of house prices and identify variations across property segments.
- Investigate relationships between property attributes and housing prices.
- Compare average property prices across different view and condition categories.
- Explore geographical housing market patterns through map-based visualization.
- Develop an interactive Tableau dashboard with dynamic filtering capabilities.
- Convert complex housing information into accessible, business-oriented visual insights.
- Support property evaluation, market comparison, and data-driven decision-making.

## 5. Interactive Tableau Dashboard Features

Developed an integrated Tableau dashboard combining multiple analytical visualizations and interactive filtering capabilities.

**Key dashboard functionalities:**

1. **Date-Based Filtering:** Explore housing information by selecting specific months and years.
2. **Calendar-Based Navigation:** Use calendar functionality to interact with date-related information.
3. **Year Built Filter:** Investigate properties across different construction-year ranges.
4. **Living Area Filter:** Analyze properties based on their residential living space.
5. **Lot Size Filter:** Explore housing properties according to land area.
6. **Time-Series Visualization:** Examine daily average house price variations.
7. **Geographical Mapping:** Explore spatial housing market patterns.
8. **Distribution Analysis:** Examine house price, bedroom, and bathroom distributions.
9. **Comparative Heatmap:** Compare average prices across property view and condition classifications.
10. **Integrated Dashboard Experience:** Consolidate multiple analytical perspectives into one interactive reporting environment.

The dashboard allows users to explore different housing market segments without relying exclusively on static reports.

## 6. Key Analytical Observations

Based on the dashboard visualizations:

- House prices exhibit variations across the displayed daily reporting period.
- The house price distribution shows a concentration across lower and mid-range price bins, with a longer tail toward higher-priced properties.
- Three- and four-bedroom properties represent prominent categories in the displayed bedroom distribution.
- Two-bathroom properties are strongly represented in the displayed bathroom distribution.
- Average house prices vary across combinations of property view and condition classifications.
- Geographical mapping provides an additional perspective for exploring the distribution of housing information.

These observations are descriptive findings from the visualized data and should not be interpreted as proof of causation or future price predictions.
